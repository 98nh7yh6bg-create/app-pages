Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026

Things keeps track of what you own. It doesn’t collect any personal data: there’s no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and I (the developer) don’t run any server that receives information from the app.

What stays on your device

Your items, categories, photos, scanned documents, attachments and settings are kept on your iPhone or iPad.

iCloud

If you’re signed in to iCloud, Things keeps your items, categories and their photos and attachments in step across your devices using your own iCloud account. They’re stored by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy, and I can’t access them.

Camera, photos and files

Things uses the camera, your photo library, the document scanner or the Files app only when you choose to add a photo, scan or file to an item. Only what you pick is added. You can change camera access anytime in the Settings app.

Reminders

If you set an expiry reminder, it’s scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to a server to deliver it. You can turn notifications off in the Settings app.

Links

Links you add to an item open in your web browser, where the website you visit has its own privacy policy.

Children

Things doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
