7 Oct 2026

1.5

Favourites, Peak Season and a lighter app

New

  • Favourites. Tap the heart on any item to save it. Your favourites get their own tab, with what’s in season now at the top, and their own widget.
  • Peak Season on Today shows what’s at its best this month, just under New This Month.
  • Get notified when your favourites reach peak season. Turn it on in Settings, under Monthly Updates.
  • Widgets can show only peak-season items, or only your favourites that are in season.
  • Your favourites and Today settings sync across your devices with iCloud.
  • Ask Siri to open an item or search for produce.
  • Support for iPhone Duo.
  • New produce: flat peaches, courgette flowers, lemongrass, spring onions and shallots.
  • Not seeing your country? Request it from the country picker.

Improved

  • About a third smaller to download, and quicker to open.
  • Smoother all round, with category filters just above the tab bar on the latest iOS.
  • Expand and collapse sections on Today, which now keeps the early, peak and late markers to Browse so it’s easier to scan.
  • More accurate nutrition and storage information, and clearer names, including jujube.
  • New welcome screens.
  • iPad improvements, including a clearer Browse tab.

Fixed

  • The search icon is back on the Browse tab.
13 Sep 2026

1.4

New countries, new artwork and iOS 27

New

  • Five new countries: Mexico, New Zealand, Poland, South Africa and South Korea.
  • 20 new items: Asian pear, cherimoya, jackfruit, guava, chayote, jícama, tomatillos, nopales, watercress, sorrel, kohlrabi, celeriac, horseradish, elderberries, currants, samphire, saltwort, sea fennel, rose petals and pecans.
  • Browse by group. Tap citrus, berries, stone fruit, melons, leafy greens, roots, brassicas, alliums or squash to see everything in it.
  • Every item now has its botanical name, where it comes from and its history, with a link to Wikipedia.
  • Widgets you can edit: show everything, only fruit or only vegetables.

Improved

  • All-new artwork. Every item is redrawn to be sharper and more accurate, and shown a little larger.
  • A refreshed design for iOS 27, with a clearer Liquid Glass tab bar, top bar and filter bar, and tidier item pages.
  • Search finds items by their local names, in any language the app supports.
  • Local names now come first.
  • Better VoiceOver support, and calmer filter animations with Reduce Motion on.
  • iPad improvements.
  • Legumes and herbs now start hidden, and Welsh names in the UK are off by default. Both can be turned back on.

Fixed

  • Widgets work with the clear Home Screen style.
  • Items without a local name no longer look cramped.

1.0

Meet In Season

The first version of In Season: what’s in season right now, wherever you are.

New

  • See which fruit and vegetables are in season in your country, and whether they’re early, at their peak or late.
  • 11 countries: Australia, Canada, France, Germany, Greece, Italy, the Netherlands, Portugal, Spain, the United Kingdom and the United States.
  • Health benefits and nutrition for every item.
  • Local names for produce in each country.
  • Show only local produce, or include imports.
  • Look up what’s in season in other countries, and what’s available all year.
  • A notification on the 1st of each month.
  • Light, dark and automatic appearance.
