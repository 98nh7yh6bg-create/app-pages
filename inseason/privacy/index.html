Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026

In Season shows which fruit and vegetables are in season. It doesn’t collect any personal data: there’s no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and I (the developer) don’t run any server that receives information from the app.

What stays on your device

Your favourites and settings, such as your country and what Today shows, are kept on your iPhone or iPad, and shared with the In Season widget on the same device.

iCloud

If you’re signed in to iCloud, In Season keeps your favourites and Today settings in step across your devices using your own iCloud account. They’re stored by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy, and I can’t access them.

Notifications

If you turn them on, In Season reminds you when new produce comes into season or a favourite is at its peak. These reminders are scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to a server to deliver them. You can turn them off in the app or in the Settings app.

Links

Learning more about a fruit or vegetable opens its Wikipedia article. Wikipedia then sees that visit, like any website, under its own privacy policy.

Children

In Season doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
