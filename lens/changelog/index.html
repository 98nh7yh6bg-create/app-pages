0.4.1TestFlight

Translation, history lock and smoother video

New

  • Lock your history and archived tabs with Face ID.
  • Swipe anywhere on a page to go back or forward, not just from the edge. It’s off by default, turn it on in Settings, switch it off for specific websites from the page menu.
  • Added Search for history and archived tabs.
  • Added app icon shortcuts on your Home Screen. Paste & Search, New Tab and New Private Tab
  • Hide Elements keeps a list of what you’ve hidden, so you can bring any of it back.

Improved

  • Improvements to Translate. You can now choose which language pages are translated into, with a flag beside each one. There’s a new Translation page in Settings where you pick it and list the languages you read, so pages in those languages don’t show Translate. You can also switch a translated page to another language from the page menu, and only languages Apple can translate are offered.
  • Improvements for video feeds like X, Instagram, TikTok or YouTube Shorts.
  • The bar above the page takes the page’s colour, as in Safari.
  • Ads and trackers are blocked on YouTube again.
  • Developer Tools has a single switch in Settings. Default off for non-dev users, so the page menu stays simple.
  • History, Tabs and Downloads clear from a trash button, and pull to refresh works on the Devices page.
  • Updates and clearer wording in Settings, including “Like Other Tabs” for Close Private Tabs.
  • Redesigned error pages.

Fixed

  • Fixed an issue that could keep the Translate option from appearing, for example on English pages when your iPhone is set to another language.
  • Editing the middle of an address no longer throws the cursor to the end, and undo, dictation and Japanese, Chinese and Korean keyboards work properly in the address bar.
  • Closing the language download sheet no longer makes it pop up again and again, and English pages are no longer mistaken for Dutch.
  • New tabs always opens right after the one they came from.
  • Reader themes style only the article and not the entire app
  • Swiping non-center tabs to close is now fixed.

0.4TestFlight

Default browser, passkeys and developer tools

Default browser, passwords and passkeys

  • Make Lens your default browser. Go to Settings → Set as Default Browser, and links from Mail, Messages and other apps open in Lens.
  • Passwords and passkeys work. iCloud Keychain fills your sign-ins, and you can sign in or sign up with a passkey.
  • Apple Pay works on websites.

Developer tools

Found in Settings → Developer Tools. Each tool has its own switch, so you can turn off the ones you don’t need.

  • Inspector: tap anything on a page to see its size, padding and margin, fonts, colours with a contrast check, tap-target size, accessibility details, attributes and CSS selector. Step out to the parent or back in to a child. Open it by holding the ••• button, or from the page menu.
  • Press and hold another element to measure the distance to it. Pause to use the page for a moment, then pick up where you left off.
  • Press and hold a link, image or selected text and choose Inspect to start right on it.
  • Font Info: select text to see its font, where it comes from, size, weight and colour. Tap any value to copy it, or see every font on the page.
  • Page Colors: see every text, background, border and icon colour a page uses, tap one to copy it, or copy them all as CSS variables.
  • Image Info: press and hold an image for its real size, format and file size.
  • Where Does This Go?: press and hold a link to see where a short link really leads, without opening it.

New

  • Request Desktop Site, in the page menu. Lens remembers your choice for each site, including its mobile address.
  • Copy as Markdown: on article pages, the share sheet copies the clean article, ready for your notes or an AI chat.
  • Print or save as PDF from the share sheet. In Reader, it prints just the article.
  • Add to Home Screen from the share sheet, to keep a site like an app.
  • Open saved web pages and link shortcuts from Files and Mail.
  • Kagi is now a search engine choice, with suggestions as you type.

Improved

  • Pull the search suggestions down and the keyboard follows your finger.
  • Low Power Mode saves more: videos that start by themselves wait for a tap.
  • Smoother scrolling, quicker launch and lighter on battery.
  • Hide Elements has round ✕ and ✓ buttons, and taps are no longer missed on pages with moving content.
  • Sites ask for your camera or microphone once, not on every visit.
  • Addresses on your own network, like 192.168.1.1, open as typed.
  • Switches are green on Mac in dark mode.

Fixed

  • Pasting no longer asks for permission.
  • Picture in Picture no longer starts when you open Control Center.
  • Back no longer gets stuck on pages that redirect.
  • Searches made in a website’s own search box show properly in History.

A faster tab switcher and the Mini Player

New

  • Get to your tabs from anywhere: pinch a page, or swipe up on the + button. Swipe left or right on + to move between open tabs.
  • The Mini Player shows what’s playing at the bottom of the screen and follows you as you browse other tabs. Turn it on in Settings.
  • Leave a video’s tab and the Mini Player keeps it ready to carry on in the background. Hold the player to jump back to its tab.
  • Select text and tap Search, next to Copy, to look it up in a new tab.
  • Hold the ••• button for a shortcut to Hide Elements.

Improved

  • Links open right next to the tab you’re on.
  • Swipe back on a tab with nowhere left to go to close it and return to where you came from.
  • Reopen Closed Tab puts the tab back where it was.
  • The Mini Player has small bars that move while something plays, steps aside for the keyboard, and takes the place of the fullscreen and Picture in Picture buttons.
  • Tabs stay centred in the switcher while the Mini Player is showing.
  • Downloads stay visible all the way into the Downloads button.
  • A refreshed app icon.

Fixed

  • Closing a tab stops its video and Picture in Picture, in private tabs too.
  • Websites’ own tab bars no longer appear in the middle of the screen.

0.2TestFlight

Meet Lens

The first beta of Lens: a fast, light and truly private browser for iPhone and iPad, built natively with Liquid Glass. No account, no ads and no data collected, in an app under 5 MB.

Search first

  • Lens opens ready to search, with suggestions as you type and your pinned sites as tiles above them.
  • Hold a suggestion to pin it, drag pins to reorder them, and give each one its own icon.
  • Choose your search engine, with a plain note on how private each one is.
  • Hold the + button or the address field to Paste and Search.

Private by design

  • Blocks ads, trackers, cookie banners and most pop-ups, with lists that update daily.
  • Removes tracking from the links you open, and HTTPS-Only mode keeps connections secure. Tap the lock in the page menu to see a site’s connection and certificate details.
  • Private tabs never touch your storage: nothing is saved, cookies live in memory and are erased when you close them. Lock them with Face ID.
  • Forget This Website clears everything one site has stored, from logins to the counters some sites use to limit free articles.
  • Lens collects nothing about you: no account, no analytics, no tracking.

Tabs that stay tidy

  • A tab switcher that snaps into place, with cards you can flick away.
  • Tabs you haven’t used in 24 hours move to the Archive, so the switcher stays clean. Choose anything from 12 hours to 30 days.
  • Hold a link to peek at it. Scroll the preview, then open it as a tab or in the background.
  • Hold the back and forward arrows to see a tab’s history.

Made for reading

  • Reader, Find on Page and page zoom.
  • Hide Elements: tap anything on a page to remove it for good. Goodbye, Shorts.
  • Translate pages on your device.
  • Fullscreen browsing: the toolbar slips away as you scroll and comes back with a tap.
  • A proper downloads manager. Downloads keep going in the background, resume if interrupted and are saved where you can find them in Files.

Works with everything else

  • iCloud Passwords, one-time codes, AutoFill and Wallet passes.
  • iCloud sync for pins, tabs, history and settings, end-to-end encrypted.
  • Handoff, plus desktop websites, menu bar items and keyboard shortcuts on iPad and Mac.
