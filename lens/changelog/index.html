0.3TestFlight

Kagi search, passkeys and smoother scrolling

New

  • Kagi is now a search engine choice, with suggestions as you type.
  • Passkeys, Apple Pay and web apps that work offline now work in Lens.
  • Print a page, or save it as a PDF, from the share sheet. In Reader, it prints just the article.
  • When a page is on the block lists, Lens says so and lets you allow that site.
  • Hold the Mini Player’s playing cue to jump to the tab that’s playing.

Improved

  • Smoother scrolling, and lighter on battery in the background.
  • New tabs open beside the one you’re on, not at the end.
  • Reopen Closed Tab puts the tab back where it was.
  • Swipe back closes any tab that has no page to go back to.
  • The Mini Player is now off until you turn it on in Settings, and it steps aside for the keyboard.
  • Sites ask for your camera or microphone once, not on every visit.
  • Addresses on your own network, like 192.168.1.1, open as typed.
  • A new app icon.

Fixed

  • Pasting no longer asks for permission.
  • Picture in Picture no longer starts when you open Control Center.
  • Closing a tab stops its sound and Picture in Picture.
  • Back no longer gets stuck on pages that redirect.
  • Searches made in a website’s own search box show properly in History.
  • Switches are green on Mac in dark mode.
Oct 2026

0.2TestFlight

The first beta

Lens is a fast, private web browser for iPhone and iPad.

New

  • Blocks ads, trackers and cookie banners, with lists that update daily.
  • Removes tracking from links you open, and HTTPS-Only mode keeps connections secure.
  • Private tabs are never saved, and you can lock them with Face ID.
  • A tab switcher that snaps like the app switcher. Pinch a page to see all your tabs.
  • Old tabs are archived automatically, so the switcher stays tidy.
  • A home screen with your pinned sites, and search suggestions as you type.
  • Link previews you can scroll, then tap to keep as a tab.
  • Reader, with Sepia and other themes.
  • Translate pages on your device.
  • Find on page, per-site zoom and fullscreen, and Hide Elements to tidy a page.
  • The Mini Player keeps sound from your tabs playing in the background, with controls a swipe away.
  • iCloud sync for pins, settings, history and open tabs, end-to-end encrypted.
  • Handoff, AutoFill, Add to Wallet passes, and downloads kept until you save them.
  • Desktop websites, menu bar items and keyboard shortcuts on iPad and Mac.
