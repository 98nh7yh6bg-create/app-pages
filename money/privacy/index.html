Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026

Money & Bills keeps your net worth history, recurring bills, clients and invoices in one place. It doesn’t collect any personal data: there’s no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and I (the developer) don’t run any server that receives information from the app.

What stays on your device

Your balances, bills, clients, projects, invoices, invoice PDFs and settings are kept on your iPhone or iPad, and shared with the app’s widget and share extension on the same device.

iCloud

If you’re signed in to iCloud, Money & Bills keeps your records and settings in step across your devices using your own iCloud account. They’re stored by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy, and I can’t access them.

Files and backups

The app only reads a file when you choose one: importing an invoice PDF, sharing a PDF to the app, or restoring a backup. Backups are exported only when you ask, to wherever you choose to save them.

Reminders

If you turn on billing reminders, they’re scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to a server to deliver them. You can turn notifications off in the app or in the Settings app.

Children

Money & Bills doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
